I'm Muhammad Abdullah, a multi-skilled creative and developer based in Lahore, Pakistan. Over the past 2+ years, I've worked across graphic design, web development, video editing, and social media management — building everything from full websites and apps to C++ projects, while helping brands grow their online presence from the ground up.
